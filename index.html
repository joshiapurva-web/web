Our Services

Specialized care, all in one place

From the first hearing screening to long-term therapy, the clinic supports the full spectrum of audiology and speech-language needs.

Hearing Tests

Comprehensive diagnostics including Audiometry, OAE, and BERA for accurate evaluation.

Hearing Aid Trial & Fitting

Trial, selection, and precise fitting of modern digital hearing aids tailored to you.

Speech Therapy

Evidence-based therapy for articulation, fluency, and language difficulties in adults.

Pediatric Speech Therapy

Gentle, play-based sessions to support language and communication in children.

Voice Therapy

Targeted treatment for hoarseness, vocal strain, and professional voice care.

Care Guidance

Clear counselling for families, follow-ups, and practical next steps after diagnosis.
